## 60–90 second walkthrough script

**0–15 seconds — The proposition**

“I focused on the connection between configuring a trip and actually traveling it. The goal is to make choices feel simple while keeping the consequences clear.”

**15–35 seconds — Stay and travel**

On the presentation page, switch Nice to Monaco.

“Here are two travelers sharing the same departure. Changing their base updates both the group price and how they get to the circuit. The breakdown distinguishes what is due today from the remaining balance.”

**35–55 seconds — Different people, different plans**

Toggle the boat off and on.

“Only Traveler 1 joins the coast boat. Traveler 2 keeps a free morning. They share a trip without needing identical plans.”

**55–75 seconds — Carry the choice forward**

Select **See these plans in the app**, then open the avatar, select Traveler 2, and close the dialog.

“Those same choices carry into the companion. Traveler 1 sees the meeting point; Traveler 2 sees an open morning. The itinerary, tickets, stay, and support remain close by.”

**75–90 seconds — Design rationale**

“The full concept also includes the website and a five-stage builder. It is a starting point for discussing your booking rules, traveler needs, and visual direction.”
